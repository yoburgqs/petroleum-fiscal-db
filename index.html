Methodology

  
How government take is calculated, key assumptions, evidence quality, and production weighting approach.


  
  

    
How Government Take is Calculated

    

      All fiscal metrics are computed on a standardized project basis: $2B deepwater capex, 25-year project life (10-year for buy-back contracts), and a 10% discount rate. Government take is the undiscounted share of gross revenue captured by the state across all fiscal instruments.
    

    
Formula

    
govt_take_pct = (royalties + income_tax + resource_rent_tax + profit_oil_state_share
               + bonuses + state_equity_carry + other_levies)
               ÷ gross_undiscounted_revenue × 100

    
9 Fiscal Mechanics

    

      

        
          
MechanicDescription

        
        
          
ConcessionRoyalty + income tax system. Contractor owns production, pays royalty off the top then taxes on profit. Most common globally (North Sea, Americas, Africa).

          
PSCProduction Sharing Contract. Cost oil recovers capex/opex; remaining profit oil split between state and contractor per contract schedule. Dominant in SE Asia, West Africa.

          
TSCTechnical Service Contract. Contractor paid a per-barrel fee; state owns all production. Iraq's dominant model post-2009.

          
PRRTPetroleum Resource Rent Tax. Cash-flow based resource rent tax triggered after threshold rate of return. Australia's primary offshore instrument.

          
RSCRisk Service Contract. Contractor recovers costs plus a margin; state retains equity. India's current model (Bid Parameter = Revenue Share).

          
Gross SplitIndonesia's post-2017 PSC reform. Eliminates cost recovery; split determined upfront by field parameters (water depth, API, location). No cost oil mechanism.

          
EPSAExploration & Production Sharing Agreement. Libya's hybrid model combining equity participation with production sharing. Four EPSA generations (I–IV).

          
Buy-backIran's primary model. Contractor finances development, transfers operatorship to NIOC, recovers costs + fee over contract term (typically 7–10 years). No equity stake.

          
Revenue ShareDirect revenue share without cost recovery mechanism. Used in select African and Latin American contracts.

The following standardizing assumptions are applied uniformly across all 71,601 contracts. Each introduces known distortion described below.

    

      

        
          
AssumptionValue UsedKnown Limitation

        
        
          
Project life25 years (10yr buy-back)Understates take for short buy-back contracts

          
Capex$2B deepwaterScale-neutral for flat-rate mechanics; affects R-factor timing for small fields

          
Opex$15–20/bblToo high for US GoM shallow; too low for North Sea brownfield

          
Discount rate10% (not risk-adjusted)No country risk premium applied — NPV comparisons across countries overstate low-risk jurisdictions

          
Tax loss carry-forwardUnlimitedUSA caps at 80% of taxable income; overstates contractor NPV in capped jurisdictions

Evidence Quality Tiers

    
Each fiscal fact in the database is assigned a confidence tier based on source type. Evidence % shown in the Screener reflects the share of a country's fiscal facts backed by A or B sources.

    

      

        
          
TierSource TypeDescription

        
        
          
APrimary legislation or regulator filingDirectly sourced from the authoritative legal instrument (e.g., Petroleum Act, Finance Act, PSC Schedule). Highest reliability.

          
BOperator annual report or verified practitioner guideSourced from an operator's disclosure (10-K, annual report) or a practitioner reference validated against primary law.

          
CSecondary aggregator (EY/IHS, KPMG, Wood Mac)Country-average estimate from a professional aggregator. May lag reforms; treat as indicative.

          
DDefault estimateNo source found. Value derived from regional average or mechanic default. Use with caution.

        
      

    

    

      Note: 43.4% of facts are bulk-linked — assigned at the country level rather than verified per individual contract. Per-contract verification is the active work frontier for high-coverage countries (Angola, Nigeria, Indonesia, Iraq-KRG).
    

  


  
  

    
Production Weighting

    

      Country-level fiscal metrics are computed as a weighted average of all contracts within that country. Two approaches are used:
    

    

      

        
          
MethodCountriesLogicLimitation

        
        
          

            Production-weighted
            21 countries
            Contract take is weighted by that contract's share of country production. Reflects the regime a typical barrel faces.
            Requires production allocation data per contract; not available for most countries.
          

          

            Equal-weighted
            164 countries
            Arithmetic average of all contracts in-country. Simple and reproducible.
            A 1-well marginal block and a 500 MMbbl giant field receive identical weight. Country averages for major producers (USA, Russia) should be interpreted cautiously.
          

        
      

    

    

      Production-weighted countries: Norway, UK, USA, Russia, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Iraq, Iran, Qatar, Libya, Algeria, Nigeria, Angola, Brazil, Venezuela, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Malaysia, Indonesia, Australia.
